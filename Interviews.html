Research Question

Why do many children in Hawassa, Ethiopia struggle to read and write, and what can be done about it?

Why This Matters

In my community, many students repeat grades multiple times and still cannot write their own names in English. They memorize letters for tests, then forget them. Reading is not part of daily life for most families here.

Methodology

I conduct interviews with students, parents, and community members. I record their stories, note the patterns, and analyze what they reveal about education in Ethiopia.
