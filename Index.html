Welcome

I am a Grade 11 student at Comboni Senior Secondary School in Hawassa, Ethiopia. This site documents my research on literacy, education, and school dropout in my community.

What I'm Working On

I am interviewing students, parents, and community members to understand why so many children in Hawassa struggle to read and write. I am also proposing a structured reading club at my school.

Why This Matters

Reading changed my life. I want to understand why it doesn't change everyone's — and what can be done about it.
