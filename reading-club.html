What I'm Building

I am proposing a structured reading club at Comboni Senior Secondary School. The club will run for 40 weeks with monthly book collections, reading levels, and reflection questionnaires.

How It Works

  • Monthly book collections on Telegram
  • Readers choose one book per month
  • Eight volunteers from Grades 9–12
  • Google Form reflection after each book
  • Digital certificates at the end of the year

Status

Proposal submitted to the Deputy Director on October 5, 2026. Awaiting approval.
